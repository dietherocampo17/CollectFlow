# CollectFlow Role and Workflow Guide

This guide documents the role-specific navigation and user-visible operations in the Docker/MySQL deployment. Screenshots below were captured from the running application using temporary QA accounts; the temporary accounts were removed after capture. Do not use these screenshots as login credentials.

## Start the application

Follow the Docker Compose instructions in the [deployment README](../README.md). The app is available at `http://localhost:8001`. The System Administrator creates real role accounts in **User administration**. No demo role passwords are shipped.

New account numbers are generated by MySQL when a Supervisor or Administrator saves an account. Existing IDs are preserved.

## Roles and capabilities

### System Administrator

- View all agency dashboards, accounts, promises, activities, follow-up tasks, payments, client portfolios, and reports.
- Create accounts and assign them to active Collector users.
- Create role accounts, associate Client users with a portfolio, activate/deactivate or delete users.
- Delete accounts, promises, payments, activities, tasks, and empty client portfolios. Deletes are audited; accounts with dependent collection data cascade safely.
- Review the audit log and change the administrator password.

### Collection Supervisor

- View agency queues, account assignments, activity timelines, promises, payments, follow-up tasks, client portfolios, reports, and strategy simulations.
- Create accounts, reassign accounts to active Collectors, update account status/balance/aging, record interactions, promises and payments, create and assign follow-up tasks, and create clients.
- Cannot manage user accounts or delete business data.

### Collector

- View only accounts assigned to the Collector, their associated promises/payments, their activity history, and their follow-up queue.
- Update assigned account status; record collection interactions; create promises and payment records; create follow-ups for assigned accounts; mark assigned follow-ups in progress or complete.
- Follow-up ownership is assigned automatically to the signed-in Collector. The assignee identity is shown read-only.
- Cannot reassign accounts, view other users' accounts, administer users, or delete records.

### Client

- View only the associated client portfolio and its accounts, metrics, and collection activity.
- Read-only. No account, promise, payment, or task mutations are exposed.

### Management

- View organization-level account metrics, reports, and the collection strategy simulator.
- Read-only; cannot change accounts, promises, payments, tasks, clients, or users.

## Workflow behavior

- Promise statuses are updated from due dates: upcoming, due, fulfilled, or broken.
- A broken promise creates an open follow-up task for the account's assigned Collector.
- Recording a payment decreases the outstanding account balance. When linked payment totals satisfy a promise, that promise becomes fulfilled.
- Admin payment deletion reverses the payment amount from the account balance and recalculates a fulfilled promise if needed.
- The interaction summary helper creates a structured local draft. A user reviews and confirms the text before it is added to the account activity timeline; it is not connected to an external AI model.
- The strategy simulator reorders the current accessible account set by aging, balance, or upcoming promises. It is a planning aid, not a collection outcome prediction.

## Access and operational notes

- The API enforces role checks in addition to hiding unavailable navigation controls.
- Collector accounts are scoped by the Collector user's full name. Assign account records to the exact full name used by that login.
- Client accounts are scoped by the user's assigned client portfolio.
- If there are no active Collector users, Supervisor/Admin account creation and task assignment are disabled with an on-screen setup hint. Create or reactivate a Collector in User administration first.
- Outbound SMS/email delivery, scheduled background execution, and bulk account import are not currently integrated.
